# GEHC-Pitch

GE Healthcare (GEHC) — Investment Pitch Summary

What is this pitch?
This is an equity research pitch arguing that GE Healthcare, one of the world's leading medical imaging companies, is significantly undervalued and presents an attractive investment opportunity over a 24-month horizon.
Current price: $61 | Base target: $79.91 (+31%) | Bull target: $108.13 (+77%)

Why is it undervalued?
When GE spun off its healthcare division, GE retained a 19.9% stake and has been slowly selling it to pay down its own debt. This has created a persistent price ceiling — rational buyers hold back while the seller overhang exists. The result is a 44% discount to medtech peers, against a historical discount of just 14%. The pitch argues only ~15-20% of that discount is genuinely justified (China exposure, margin gap), leaving the rest as an unearned penalty set to unwind.

What makes the business strong?

$21.8bn order backlog with book-to-bill of 1.07× — guaranteed near-term revenue
~50% of revenue is recurring (service contracts, consumables, software)
Scanners have a 7–10 year replacement cycle with multi-million dollar switching costs — customers don't leave
AI software (AIR Recon DL) already at 45% attach rate vs management's own 30% target, at 85% gross margins
Pharmaceutical Diagnostics (contrast agents/PET tracers) is the fastest-growing, highest-margin segment (~30% EBIT)


The three catalysts the market is missing

Margin expansion — Street models miss the compounding effect of AI software attach and the Flyrcado cardiac PET drug scaling from $50m to $590m in revenue by FY30
Trapped China cash — ~$400–500m is locked in unpaid Chinese hospital receivables due to Beijing's anti-corruption crackdown. This is a timing issue, not a credit loss — Beijing has authorised RMB 4.4T in bonds to clear backlogs
GE overhang clearing — GE is expected to fully exit its stake by 2026, removing the price ceiling that has suppressed the stock since spin-off
